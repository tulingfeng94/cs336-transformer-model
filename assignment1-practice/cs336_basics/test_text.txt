Once upon a time, there was a girl. She was very small, just three years old.
The girl had lots of clothes. She wanted to manage them, to keep them tidy. But it was difficult for her, because she was so small.
One day, the girl found one of her clothes. But it was dead! She cried, because she was sad.
But the girl was brave. She managed to find all the other clothes, and tidy them up. In the end, everything was nice and tidy.
The girl was very happy.

One day, a little girl named Sue was very sleepy. She went to the store to get some milk for her mom. As she walked, she saw a cat. The cat was also very sleepy. Sue said, "Hi, sleepy cat! Do you want to come with me to get milk?"
The cat said, "Yes, I can help you." So, they both started to skip to the store. They were happy and laughed as they skipped. They got the milk and started to skip back home.
But then, something unexpected happened. They saw a big dog who wanted the milk. The dog said, "Give me the milk or I will be mean to you!" Sue and the cat were scared, but they knew they had to be brave. Sue said, "No, we will not give you the milk. You should be nice and not mean." The dog thought about it and said, "You are right. I am sorry." The dog, Sue, and the cat all became friends and skipped back home together. The moral of the story is to be brave and stand up for what is right.

Once upon a time, there was a big ship. The ship had many people on it. They all wanted to go to a fun place across the water. One day, a man on the ship saw a bird. The bird could whistle. The man thought it was funny and wanted to whistle too.
The man tried to whistle, but he could not do it. He felt foolish. He tried and tried, but he just could not whistle like the bird. The other people on the ship laughed at him. They said, "You cannot whistle. You are not a bird."
The man felt sad, but he did not give up. He kept trying to whistle. One day, he finally did it! He could whistle just like the bird. The people on the ship stopped laughing at him. They said, "Good job! You did it!"
The moral of the story is to never give up. Even if you feel foolish, keep trying. You can do anything if you try hard enough.
